# NOTICE — publishing boundary

## Intended for public / open distribution

- `README.md`, `AGENT_SYNOPSIS.md`, `METHOD.md`, this file  
- `index.json` and `runs/*/METRICS.md` + `runs/*/metrics.json`

## Do not overclaim

- **Node2 alone** is not certified production-ready by these pilots.  
- Results are **single-seed** (one short steel-style brief → 2000 cases).  
- Corruptions are **synthetic templates**.  
- Cite as **pilot / validation**, not universal SOTA.

## Hold back unless explicitly scrubbed

- Raw SQLite eval DBs (`sv_corpus.db`) once seeds include proprietary Kit, customer, or prospectus text  
- Private seed dumps (Steel Market Pulse internal extracts, client rationales, session transcripts)  
- LAN hostnames, keys, or internal-only Modelfile secrets  

Public seed example used for pilots is a short illustrative steel market brief suitable for method demos; replace with your own multi-seed set for product claims.
